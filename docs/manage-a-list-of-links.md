**Osmium Links** keeps a list of external links, such as partners or brands, for your site's design to show.

## Add a link

1. Press **Add Link**.
2. Enter the **Name** and **Web address**.
3. Add a short **Description** if you want one.
4. Set a **Group** to list links together, for example Brands. Links with the same group are listed together.
5. **Order** sets the position, and a lower number shows first.
6. Optionally upload a **Logo**.
7. Press **Save**.

## Change or remove one

Use **Edit** on its row. **Delete** moves it to **Deleted Links**, and **Purge All** there removes them for good.
